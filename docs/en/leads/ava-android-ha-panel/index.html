Ava: turn older Android phones/tablets into HA panels and BLE gateways

The author releases an Ava variant for Android 7–16 including 32-bit devices, turning spares into Home Assistant voice/control panels with a BLE Proxy gateway.

L39 · E0 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author releases an Ava variant for Android 7–16 including 32-bit devices, turning spares into Home Assistant voice/control panels with a BLE Proxy gateway.

Practical readiness

A trial path for spare devices that can still install the APK; low-end support is an author claim.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Spare Android 7–16 devices able to install the author release
  • Panel and BLE-coverage experiments on a Home Assistant LAN

Not established or unsuitable

  • Aged batteries that cannot be powered safely
  • Critical home control requiring proven multi-year stability

Why this lead is useful

Combines display, voice and BLE roles into one old-phone route.

Should you continue?

Pilot on a spare device and observe permissions/power; retain another control path for critical functions.

Why it remains E0

This is a 2026 community beta; long-running background behavior, microphone variation, BLE stability, privacy and updates lack broad reproduction.

Next review step

Test discovery, control, BLE, reboot recovery, permissions and power on one Android 7 and one newer device.

Risk and stop condition

Check battery health and charge limits before continuous power, and sideload only from the author's release link.
