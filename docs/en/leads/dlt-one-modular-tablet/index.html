Decide before you build

  • Current stage The project proposes open mechanical and electrical module standards so tablet boards, displays, batteries and peripherals can be upgraded and reused across form factors.
  • Practical readiness Useful as a repairable-tablet design reference, but it cannot modularize an arbitrary old tablet.
  • User value Provides a modular design approach and iteration record aimed at preventing complete-device disposal.
  • Follow-up recommendation Continue for platform research; use a model-specific project for one existing tablet.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • People developing open tablets or industrial terminals
  • Research into cross-generation display, battery and peripheral reuse

Not established or unsuitable

  • Direct upgrades of existing iPads or Android tablets
  • Quick builds without PCB and mechanical-design capability

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • Why it remains E0 It is primarily a new hardware platform rather than a conversion kit for an existing tablet, and PCB/mechanical compatibility is still iterative.
  • Next review step Confirm current manufacturable files, assembled revisions, module matrix and which salvaged parts are actually reusable.
  • Risk and stop condition Battery management, voltage and connectors cannot be interchanged merely because modules fit mechanically.
