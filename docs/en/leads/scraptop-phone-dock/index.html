Decide before you build

  • Current stage The project aims to retain an old laptop display, keyboard and enclosure while using USB OTG, DisplayLink and DVI/LVDS conversion around a phone as the compute core.
  • Practical readiness Its main value is the integration route and difficulty map, not a universal conversion board.
  • User value Concentrates the hardest laptop-part reuse problems: keyboard and display interfaces.
  • Follow-up recommendation Continue for interface research; prove one exact combination before pursuing a practical dock.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Specific laptops with intact enclosure, keyboard and LCD but failed mainboards
  • Developers willing to solve layered video and USB control

Not established or unsuitable

  • One-cable support for arbitrary phones and laptops
  • Machines with unknown battery, LCD or cable condition

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • Why it remains E0 The DisplayLink/LVDS chain is complex and lacks a completed acceptance result; laptop cables and controllers are not standardized.
  • Next review step Determine whether a usable combination was completed, including exact panel, keyboard controller and video-chain latency.
  • Risk and stop condition Remove or separately evaluate old batteries; CCFL display inverters may produce high voltage.
