Decide before you build

  • Current stage The tutorial measures salvaged VU-meter sensitivity and compares PWM and DAC drive methods for Raspberry Pi load or audio indication.
  • Practical readiness Can turn old audio meters into slow status displays, with beginner-oriented analysis.
  • User value Encourages measuring a salvaged part before wiring it by appearance.
  • Follow-up recommendation Continue if sensitivity can be measured and limited; preserve rare complete equipment nondestructively.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Separately removable analog VU or low-voltage meters
  • Raspberry Pi PWM/DAC status output

Not established or unsuitable

  • Direct 3.3V/5V connection at unknown sensitivity
  • Precision or fast measurement

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • Why it remains E0 Meter movements vary widely; there is no universal current limit and long-term mechanical wear is untested.
  • Next review step Measure full-scale voltage/current for the exact movement, then test limiting, calibration and power-off return.
  • Risk and stop condition Fully remove the meter from original mains audio circuitry and test with a low-voltage current-limited supply.
