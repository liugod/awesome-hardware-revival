About KitAgain

Evidence, scoring, and contribution rules in one place.

Evidence levels

  • E0 Discovery only; exact model, installation, and recovery still need review.
  • E1 Source, deployment, or device-compatibility documentation reviewed.
  • E2 Author hardware claim or demonstration available.
  • E3 Verified independent reproduction available.

Evidence levels are independent of revival stars: they neither enter the weighted score nor cap it.

Revival stars

DimensionWeight
Feasibility / ease (easier is higher)25%
Cost (lower is higher)15%
Documentation and community activity20%
Practical value20%
Bricking and security risk (lower risk is higher)20%
  • Each source-backed dimension receives 1–5 points; an unevidenced dimension is stored as null, never replaced by a neutral 3.
  • Only evidenced dimensions have their original weights proportionally renormalized to 100%.
  • A total requires at least 3 evidenced dimensions; otherwise it displays ‘Not yet rated’ and sorts after every rated entry.
  • The weighted average is rounded to the nearest 0.5 star, with exact ties rounded upward.

Contribution guide

  • Include project ID, exact model, hardware revision, firmware, and region.
  • Include upstream revision, operation date, and original instruction links.
  • Keep successes, failures, recovery outcomes, and verifiable evidence.
  • Do not publish unique serial numbers, accounts, tokens, or secrets.
