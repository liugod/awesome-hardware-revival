PhoneBlox: add modular USB and battery backplanes to an older Android phone

The project designs printed housings, pogo-pin PCBs, USB 2.0 and swappable battery modules for an older Android phone, with several mechanical and Gerber files uploaded.

L22 · E0 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The project designs printed housings, pogo-pin PCBs, USB 2.0 and swappable battery modules for an older Android phone, with several mechanical and Gerber files uploaded.

Practical readiness

A modular-back prototype for one phone, not a universal Phonebloks ecosystem.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Android-phone experiments with accessible USB and battery points
  • Developers able to adapt PCBs, FPCs and printed housings

Not established or unsuitable

  • Tool-free support for arbitrary modern phones
  • Directly interchanging batteries across phone models

Why this lead is useful

Provides mechanical and PCB files closer to fabrication than a concept rendering.

Should you continue?

Continue with a spare phone and power-design review; do not use a primary phone as the first sample.

Why it remains E0

There is no multi-model matrix; solder points, USB pins, battery protection, enclosure fit and finished acceptance remain device-specific.

Next review step

Confirm the author's exact phone, V1/V2 file mapping, USB OTG and battery charge/discharge protection.

Risk and stop condition

Do not bypass original battery protection; pogo misalignment or reversed power can damage the phone and cell.
