Decide before you build

  • Current stage The project designs printed housings, pogo-pin PCBs, USB 2.0 and swappable battery modules for an older Android phone, with several mechanical and Gerber files uploaded.
  • Practical readiness A modular-back prototype for one phone, not a universal Phonebloks ecosystem.
  • User value Provides mechanical and PCB files closer to fabrication than a concept rendering.
  • Follow-up recommendation Continue with a spare phone and power-design review; do not use a primary phone as the first sample.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Android-phone experiments with accessible USB and battery points
  • Developers able to adapt PCBs, FPCs and printed housings

Not established or unsuitable

  • Tool-free support for arbitrary modern phones
  • Directly interchanging batteries across phone models

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • Why it remains E0 There is no multi-model matrix; solder points, USB pins, battery protection, enclosure fit and finished acceptance remain device-specific.
  • Next review step Confirm the author's exact phone, V1/V2 file mapping, USB OTG and battery charge/discharge protection.
  • Risk and stop condition Do not bypass original battery protection; pogo misalignment or reversed power can damage the phone and cell.
