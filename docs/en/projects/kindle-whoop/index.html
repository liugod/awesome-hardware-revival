Practical decision notes

  • What was holding it back An old Kindle's e-ink display still works, but the original system limits live information display beyond reading.
  • What it can do afterwards WHOOP data is rendered as an image and displayed on a jailbroken device, with scheduled wake and sleep attempted.
  • Assessment The author's hardware evidence is limited to a Kindle Paperwhite 2, firmware 5.10.3, and one verified wake, connect, update and sleep cycle. The project provides AI-assisted configuration documents for deployment with Codex or Claude. This does not mean AI runs locally on the Kindle or prove that all code was written by AI. One successful cycle cannot establish weeks of battery life.
  • A good fit if People with a matching old Kindle, a WHOOP data source and willingness to manage KUAL/linkss and service configuration.
  • Skip this route if People on firmware without confirmed jailbreak support, requiring fully measured long battery life, or unwilling to maintain external data services.

Checks after the build

  • Verify PW2 and firmware; do not generalize the example to any Kindle.
  • Start with manual refresh, then test scheduled wake and sleep.
  • Record power consumption, failed retries and networking over time, adding multi-day reproduction evidence.
