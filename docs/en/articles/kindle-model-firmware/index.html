Identify your Kindle model and firmware before modifying it

Confirm the exact device and installed firmware before considering KOReader or another modification. A family name such as “Paperwhite” is not enough to select a jailbreak route.

C01 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

1. Identify the device without publishing its full serial number

On the Kindle, record the device name, generation when shown, and software version under Settings → Device Options → Device Info. Menu labels can vary. Compare its features and generation with Amazon's identification page; “Kindle” or “Paperwhite” alone does not establish compatibility.

If you use the KindleModding wizard, read the first eight characters of the serial on your own device and enter them only into that tool. Do not put a full serial number, account details, or an unredacted device-info screenshot in a public help post. Model, generation and firmware are enough to begin a discussion.

2. Separate installed firmware from an available update

Write down the software version shown by Device Info and the date you checked it. Amazon's update page can help confirm the device generation and official update path; its latest listed release is not evidence of what is installed on your device.

Do not update first and look for a modification method later. Available methods change with model and firmware. The KindleModding wizard asks for device and firmware, then other conditions such as registration and lock-screen ads. If the route does not match your exact combination, stop at read-only research rather than extending another model's result.

3. Choose a route for the actual goal

If your goal is only to keep reading your current books, first decide whether any modification is needed. For KOReader, read its current Kindle installation guide and check device, launcher and prerequisites. A successful jailbreak alone does not establish that KOReader works.

WinterBreak is one possible jailbreak prerequisite, not a reader and not a universal route for every Kindle and firmware. Use the current wizard to choose a method, then read that method's original instructions, exceptions and recovery notes. Do not assume the firmware range in an old tutorial still applies.

Stop conditions and records before changing anything

Back up personal files and configuration. Record installed firmware, intended use, upstream tool version, source links and a possible recovery route. The KindleModding FAQ describes ordering requirements for resetting, updating and removing a jailbreak; a factory reset is not a universal rollback.

If model, firmware, prerequisites or recovery are unclear, do not execute a step that changes the system yet. This catalog has not performed Kindle hardware tests and does not certify compatibility for a specific device.
