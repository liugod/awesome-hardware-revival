Replace an original iPad logic board with Raspberry Pi CM4

The author designs a CM4 replacement board for a 2010 iPad to reuse the enclosure, battery, touch, LCD, antennas, speaker and buttons, with a reported booting system.

L14 · E0 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author designs a CM4 replacement board for a 2010 iPad to reuse the enclosure, battery, touch, LCD, antennas, speaker and buttons, with a reported booting system.

Practical readiness

Advanced builders may reuse much of the original structure for Linux/Android, while no mature consumer kit exists.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • The author's 2010 first-generation iPad scope
  • Developers capable of fine-pitch soldering and PCB debug

Not established or unsuitable

  • Applying directly to other iPad generations
  • Users seeking a software-only route to modern apps

Why this lead is useful

Unusually aims to retain many original components instead of using only the enclosure.

Should you continue?

Continue if board review, battery work and prototype cost are acceptable; others should wait for stable hardware.

Why it remains E0

Custom PCB, display and battery management remain complex; a complete BOM, long-term battery safety and all peripherals are not packaged for ordinary users.

Next review step

Review the latest PCB revision, manufacturing files, LCD/touch drivers, charge protection and system image.

Risk and stop condition

The aged lithium battery and custom power board need separate validation; a booting CM4 does not prove safe charging.
