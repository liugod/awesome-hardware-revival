Decide before you build

  • User value A low-complexity way to reuse common enclosures and interfaces from electronic waste.
  • Follow-up recommendation Worth doing after low-voltage and insulation checks; repair the whole router first if it remains viable.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Failed low-voltage routers with intact cases
  • Arduino, sensor or doorbell-interface prototypes

Not established or unsuitable

  • Retaining original router networking function
  • Connecting an unknown original power board directly to the new project

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • Why it remains E0 This reuses the mechanics and simple parts rather than reviving the router board; PCB isolation and mounting vary by enclosure.
  • Next review step Verify connector polarity, LED current limiting, board retention, openings and long-term power/battery design.
  • Risk and stop condition Fully remove or isolate unknown original power circuits and prevent loose boards from shorting.
