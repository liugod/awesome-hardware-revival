DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author uses an original Xbox Duke controller with a broken cable as an enclosure for a Raspberry Pi, battery, HDMI output and controls for retro games or streaming.

Practical readiness

The enclosure and controls offer a portable-console prototype, but completion is insufficient.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Xbox Duke controllers with failed cables but intact shell/buttons
  • Raspberry Pi systems intended to plug into a TV over HDMI

Not established or unsuitable

  • A nondestructive route retaining original Xbox-controller function
  • A handheld with its own screen

Why this lead is useful

Shows that the oversized controller shell can contain a whole computer and battery.

Should you continue?

Continue as a personal mechanical build; use a mature handheld platform for immediate gaming.

Why it remains E0

Only two logs are present; buttons, power, cooling, mapping and a finished result are not fully documented.

Next review step

Look for final wiring, image, runtime, shutdown control and verified gameplay input.

Risk and stop condition

A lithium cell, boost board and sealed enclosure need overcurrent and thermal testing.
