EXACT-MODEL SOURCE REVIEWED

Hardware

ESP8266 with 4 MB flash, one relay, button, LEDs and CSE7766 metering.

Installation route

Tuya-convert is not supported; remove the cover, use the serial header and hold the button at power-on for bootloader mode.

Expected result

Provides local plug control plus current, voltage, power, power factor and energy readings.

Boundary

Upstream requires board: esp12e to expose all 4 MB and warns that a web server may destabilize the slower ESP8266.

Recovery and stop condition Keep the relay ALWAYS_OFF for initial flashing and change power-restore behavior only through OTA; never attach a load while powered by the programmer.
