EXACT-MODEL SOURCE REVIEWED

Hardware

ESP32, 8 MB flash, five PWM channels, 8720 Ethernet and multiplexed five-switch inputs.

Installation route

Flash through the Pro add-on interface with GPIO0 grounded at reset; upstream explicitly says not to connect the +12 V programming pin or power other connectors with 12 V.

Expected result

Controls five monochrome channels or one RGBWW strip while retaining Ethernet and physical inputs.

Boundary

NTC, voltage-monitor and some metering pins remain unknown on the page, so PM is not a complete implementation.

Recovery and stop condition Back up and flash with loads and 12 V disconnected; stop if the intended use requires the unmapped metering or temperature functions.
