OpenBeken flashing at a glance

This page helps you assess BK7231 Tuya devices for local MQTT and Home Assistant and check what is needed before flashing. Chip support, including BK7231T and BK7231N, follows the official OpenBeken repository; verify your exact chip and board. OTA updates are available after installation, but initial flashing needs a suitable method; bricking is possible, so keep original flash and calibration backups and confirm a recovery procedure first.
