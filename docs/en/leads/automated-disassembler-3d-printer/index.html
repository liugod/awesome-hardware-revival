DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The team mounts a soldering iron and camera on a home 3D-printer motion system, using vision to find joints and attempt automated component removal.

Practical readiness

A research platform for turning an old printer into a desoldering experiment, not an automatic recycling machine.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • FDM printers with functioning motion systems
  • Powered-off and discharged simple hand-soldered boards

Not established or unsuitable

  • Automatic processing of complex multilayer production boards
  • Unattended high-temperature operation

Why this lead is useful

Connects component reuse, vision and a salvaged motion platform in an observable research path.

Should you continue?

Continue with lab safety and algorithm skills; use hand tools for occasional component recovery.

Why it remains E0

It remains experimental and targets hand-soldered boards; recognition accuracy, temperature control, fumes and mass-produced electronics are unresolved.

Next review step

Verify runnable code, camera calibration, temperature control, removal success and component-damage measurements.

Risk and stop condition

Hot tools, fumes, motion and charged capacitors require isolation, guarding and extraction.
