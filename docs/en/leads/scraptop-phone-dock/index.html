DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The project aims to retain an old laptop display, keyboard and enclosure while using USB OTG, DisplayLink and DVI/LVDS conversion around a phone as the compute core.

Practical readiness

Its main value is the integration route and difficulty map, not a universal conversion board.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Specific laptops with intact enclosure, keyboard and LCD but failed mainboards
  • Developers willing to solve layered video and USB control

Not established or unsuitable

  • One-cable support for arbitrary phones and laptops
  • Machines with unknown battery, LCD or cable condition

Why this lead is useful

Concentrates the hardest laptop-part reuse problems: keyboard and display interfaces.

Should you continue?

Continue for interface research; prove one exact combination before pursuing a practical dock.

Why it remains E0

The DisplayLink/LVDS chain is complex and lacks a completed acceptance result; laptop cables and controllers are not standardized.

Next review step

Determine whether a usable combination was completed, including exact panel, keyboard controller and video-chain latency.

Risk and stop condition

Remove or separately evaluate old batteries; CCFL display inverters may produce high voltage.
