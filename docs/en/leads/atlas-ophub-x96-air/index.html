Exact-model review

  • Hardware X96 Air listed under S905X3, with a community report for the Q1000/QCA9377 variant.
  • Installation route Choose the DTB by network chip and validate Ethernet, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth from external media before installation.
  • Expected result A specific Q1000/QCA9377 variant has a community report of all three network functions working.
  • Boundary The result does not cover every X96 Air; Q1000 and other network/memory revisions must be separated.
  • Recovery and stop condition Keep known DTBs and the stock Android image; do not write eMMC when the network chip does not match.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

Not established or unsuitable

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • What E1 still does not prove An exact-model configuration or maintainer record was read, but KitAgain did not flash, measure or run the hardware long-term, and upstream evidence cannot guarantee an identical current retail revision.
  • Next review step Match the physical model, PCB and controller against the source, then begin with the lowest-risk validation step. Keep known DTBs and the stock Android image; do not write eMMC when the network chip does not match.
  • Risk and stop condition Back up the stock Android and boot partitions. A wrong device tree may lose display, network or boot; do not overwrite eMMC before external-media testing.
