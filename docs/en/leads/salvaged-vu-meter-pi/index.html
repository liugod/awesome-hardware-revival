DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The tutorial measures salvaged VU-meter sensitivity and compares PWM and DAC drive methods for Raspberry Pi load or audio indication.

Practical readiness

Can turn old audio meters into slow status displays, with beginner-oriented analysis.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Separately removable analog VU or low-voltage meters
  • Raspberry Pi PWM/DAC status output

Not established or unsuitable

  • Direct 3.3V/5V connection at unknown sensitivity
  • Precision or fast measurement

Why this lead is useful

Encourages measuring a salvaged part before wiring it by appearance.

Should you continue?

Continue if sensitivity can be measured and limited; preserve rare complete equipment nondestructively.

Why it remains E0

Meter movements vary widely; there is no universal current limit and long-term mechanical wear is untested.

Next review step

Measure full-scale voltage/current for the exact movement, then test limiting, calibration and power-off return.

Risk and stop condition

Fully remove the meter from original mains audio circuitry and test with a low-voltage current-limited supply.
