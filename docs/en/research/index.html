Evidence before revival.

Traceable sources and explicit unknowns.

What inclusion means

E0: 347 public community leads. E1: source and deployment documentation reviewed, 50 entries. E2: author hardware claims or demonstrations, 3. E3: verified independent reproduction, 0. Catalog hands-on hardware tests: 0.

How we review

We search original repositories, project forums, author sites, Codeberg, GitLab, Hackaday, Instructables, install documentation, model tables and original social posts by device and failure mode. Source URLs and review dates are retained; search snippets and reposts are leads only. Unknown model, firmware or region requirements stay unknown. An unarchived project is not proof of continued usability.

AI relationships and project families

AI functionality, documented AI-assisted development/setup, AI-adjacent uses and traditional infrastructure are separate categories. Successor versions in one family are not independent reproductions; platforms and prerequisite tools are not completed modification cases.

Evidence gaps

There are no long-term power, stability or recovery tests, and costs are not priced. Difficulty is editorial judgment. X reading covers two case families; neither exhaustive search nor video verification is claimed.

Translation discipline

Both languages share stable IDs and factual fields. Translations record a source-content hash and must be reviewed when the source changes. Translation dates are not fresh upstream verification dates.
