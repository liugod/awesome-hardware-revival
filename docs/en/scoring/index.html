1–5 revival-value stars

Five weighted dimensions, half-star rounding, and independent evidence grades.

What the stars answer

It compares the conditions for putting a revival route into practice. It is not a compatibility guarantee, safety certification, or success rate. E0–E3 separately describes evidence depth and neither enters nor caps the stars.

Five dimensions and weights

DimensionWeight
Feasibility / ease (easier is higher)25%
Cost (lower is higher)15%
Documentation and community activity20%
Practical value20%
Bricking and security risk (lower risk is higher)20%

Calculation

Each dimension receives 1–5 points: easier feasibility, lower cost, better docs/community, greater practical value, and lower bricking/security risk score higher. The weighted average is rounded to the nearest 0.5 star, with exact ties rounded upward.

How unknowns are handled

When comparable facts are unavailable, a neutral 3 is used and recorded as unknown-neutral or unreviewed-neutral in JSON/CSV component_basis. It is a calculation placeholder, not verified average performance. The 147 E0 repository candidates use only existing metadata for provisional stars and are all marked To verify.

What is deliberately excluded

  • The E0–E3 evidence grade itself
  • Repository stars, likes, views, and search popularity
  • Unverified success rates or compatibility promises
  • Treating a recent push as proof that hardware works

Coverage and downloads

All 547 / 547 public routes have stars. The unified JSON/CSV stores total stars, weighted average, five raw component scores, component basis, independent evidence grade, review-needed state, source URL, and Chinese/English detail URLs.
