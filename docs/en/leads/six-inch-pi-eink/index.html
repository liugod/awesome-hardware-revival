Six-inch Kindle display for Raspberry Pi: incomplete prototype

The goal was a Raspberry Pi interface for a surplus Kindle panel. The author records that two PCB revisions contained errors and were unusable, then switched to another project.

L07 · E0 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The project stopped at a failed prototype: two PCB revisions were unusable and the author moved toward another adapter.

Practical readiness

There is no buildable accepted result; it is a failure record, ED060SC4 research entry and project relationship map.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Developers researching ED060SC4 or similar e-paper interfaces
  • Studying direct Raspberry Pi panel drive, PCB design and low-power terminal concepts
  • Finding adjacent routes such as L06

Not established or unsuitable

  • Any claimed working Raspberry Pi and Kindle-panel combination
  • Ordering, patching or connecting either failed PCB revision
  • A completed solar laptop, enclosure, keyboard or battery-life goal

Why this lead is useful

Records which boards failed, why work stopped and where the author turned next, preventing repeated purchases of a known-bad design.

Should you continue?

Use only for research and failure review; reproduce only after a successor revision and independent working evidence appear.

Why it remains E0

There is no reproducible successful board, final BOM or acceptance result. Its value is preserving failure evidence and project relationships.

Next review step

Upgrade only if a successor board or verifiable revision appears; link it to L06 and do not present it as a successful build.

Risk and stop condition

Do not order or connect the failed PCB design without checking a successor and the exact panel electrical requirements.
