How this changes the device

What was holding it back

The aim is to take over the XiaoAI audio path rather than bridge only through cloud conversation records.

What it can do afterwards

Patched firmware and a client on specific speakers send audio to external processing services.

Documentation distinguishes LX06 from OH2P. The LX06 route involves disassembly and Micro USB; OH2P instructions use Type-C. Never apply one model's wiring or image to the other. The official dual-partition recovery approach may help only if the original partition remains intact. The archived repository should be treated as a historical engineering project whose dependencies you may need to maintain yourself.

A good fit if

Experimenters who can identify the exact model, understand firmware partitions and prepare recovery materials.

Skip this route if

Owners of their only daily-use speaker who cannot accept downtime or cannot confirm model and firmware.

Checks after the build

  • Record model and firmware before flashing; confirm original-partition backups and the recovery path.
  • Verify audio capture and playback before connecting AI services.
  • Check connection recovery after reboot and retain a configuration that can be rolled back.

Use this as your acceptance checklist; KitAgain has not completed these hardware tests hands-on.
