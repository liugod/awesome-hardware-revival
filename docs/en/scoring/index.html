1–5 revival-value stars

Source-backed dimensions only, renormalized weights, and independent evidence grades.

What the stars answer

It compares the conditions for putting a revival route into practice. It is not a compatibility guarantee, safety certification, or success rate. E0–E3 separately describes evidence depth and neither enters nor caps the stars.

Five dimensions and weights

DimensionWeight
Feasibility / ease (easier is higher)25%
Cost (lower is higher)15%
Documentation and community activity20%
Practical value20%
Bricking and security risk (lower risk is higher)20%

Calculation

Each source-backed dimension receives 1–5 points. Only the original weights of evidenced dimensions are proportionally renormalized to 100% before averaging. The result is rounded to the nearest 0.5 star, with exact ties rounded upward.

How unknowns are handled

Unevidenced dimensions remain null: no neutral 3 is imputed and their weights do not enter the denominator. With fewer than three evidenced dimensions, both the total and weighted average are null; the page shows “Not yet rated” and sorts the entry after every rated item.

What is deliberately excluded

  • The E0–E3 evidence grade itself
  • Repository stars, likes, views, and search popularity
  • Unverified success rates or compatibility promises
  • Treating a recent push as proof that hardware works
