Exact-model review

  • Hardware Shelly 1 Gen1 with ESP8266, 2 MB flash, one dry-contact relay and exposed programming header.
  • Installation route Use a 3.3 V serial adapter capable of at least 350 mA and ground GPIO0 for flashing mode.
  • Expected result Converts Shelly 1 into a local Tasmota dry-contact controller.
  • Boundary Upstream warns about V1/V2/V3 differences and an incorrect mains-terminal drawing in the V2 user guide.
  • Recovery and stop condition Never connect AC and serial simultaneously, verify the 12 V jumper, and save the original template before flashing.
  • Upstream revision 2f25844d2266c0b58be60972cbdd2ae1ce3bf4b1
