Decide before you build

  • Current stage The author adds control electronics and lighting to an old GTE rotary phone, using dial pulses for colors and the hook switch to select odd/even positions.
  • Practical readiness Reuses the mechanical dial and enclosure as an interactive lighting object.
  • User value A compact example of preserving tactile mechanics while giving the object a new interaction.
  • Follow-up recommendation Continue if dial contacts can be identified and isolated; prefer nondestructive work on collectible complete phones.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Pulse-dial phones with intact mechanics
  • Low-voltage interactive pieces retaining the original appearance

Not established or unsuitable

  • Continued ordinary telephone service
  • Devices not fully isolated from outside lines

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • Why it remains E0 The page is an author showcase with limited circuit files and no broad interface guide for other rotary phones.
  • Next review step Identify the controller, dial-pulse input, power design and complete wiring files.
  • Risk and stop condition Disconnect completely from telephone lines and ringing voltage; never attach the modified unit to an active public network.
