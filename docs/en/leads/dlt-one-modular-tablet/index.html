DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The project proposes open mechanical and electrical module standards so tablet boards, displays, batteries and peripherals can be upgraded and reused across form factors.

Practical readiness

Useful as a repairable-tablet design reference, but it cannot modularize an arbitrary old tablet.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • People developing open tablets or industrial terminals
  • Research into cross-generation display, battery and peripheral reuse

Not established or unsuitable

  • Direct upgrades of existing iPads or Android tablets
  • Quick builds without PCB and mechanical-design capability

Why this lead is useful

Provides a modular design approach and iteration record aimed at preventing complete-device disposal.

Should you continue?

Continue for platform research; use a model-specific project for one existing tablet.

Why it remains E0

It is primarily a new hardware platform rather than a conversion kit for an existing tablet, and PCB/mechanical compatibility is still iterative.

Next review step

Confirm current manufacturable files, assembled revisions, module matrix and which salvaged parts are actually reusable.

Risk and stop condition

Battery management, voltage and connectors cannot be interchanged merely because modules fit mechanically.
