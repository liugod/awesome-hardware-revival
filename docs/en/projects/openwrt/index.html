Practical decision notes

  • What was holding it back An old router's vendor firmware may lack desired features or no longer receive updates.
  • What it can do afterwards OpenWrt on an exactly supported hardware revision can provide routing, access-point or other networking roles.
  • Assessment A mature project does not mean a particular old model still supports the current release. Brand, appearance and retail name are insufficient for choosing an image: verify hardware revision, flash and RAM. First-install and upgrade images may differ. Recovery depends on the bootloader, serial access or vendor recovery mode. Do not experiment on the home's only network gateway without a confirmed recovery route.
  • A good fit if People with backup networking who can verify device revisions and recovery documentation.
  • Skip this route if People with tiny-flash devices, no exact model entry, or no tolerance for temporary network loss.

Checks after the build

  • Check the exact device page, hardware revision, image type and checksum.
  • Back up configuration and device-specific partitions; confirm recovery tools.
  • Test WAN/LAN, wireless and rebooting. One successful connection is not a stability test.
