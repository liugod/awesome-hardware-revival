What changes for this device

Before

Some robot vacuums depend on vendor-cloud control while their mechanical parts and sensors still work.

After

A local service replaces cloud interfaces on supported devices with the necessary access.

Valetudo explicitly is not generic replacement firmware. Its support list is exhaustive; do not assume an unlisted model works. Even Xiaomi 1C units have different hardware revisions, and only specified internal models are supported. Xiaomi V1 production dates also affect whether disassembly is required. Removing cloud dependence does not add missing vendor mapping or hardware capabilities.

A good fit for

People with an exact model on the list who will prepare model-specific tools and recovery methods.

Not a good fit for

People with unlisted models or wanting to fix physical faults such as batteries or motors.

Proposed reproduction checks

  • Check the internal model, production batch and corresponding root instructions.
  • Test cleaning and local control with internet access disconnected.
  • Check maps, docking, persistent configuration and recovery conditions.

These hardware checks have not been performed by this catalog.
