Shelly 1: flash Tasmota for local control

The goal is to configure Tasmota for Shelly 1 so its switch, light, plug or sensor can be controlled locally through the web UI, MQTT or a home-automation system.

EXACT-MODEL SOURCE REVIEWED

Hardware

Shelly 1 Gen1 with ESP8266, 2 MB flash, one dry-contact relay and exposed programming header.

Installation route

Use a 3.3 V serial adapter capable of at least 350 mA and ground GPIO0 for flashing mode.

Expected result

Converts Shelly 1 into a local Tasmota dry-contact controller.

Boundary

Upstream warns about V1/V2/V3 differences and an incorrect mains-terminal drawing in the V2 user guide.

Recovery and stop condition Never connect AC and serial simultaneously, verify the 12 V jumper, and save the original template before flashing.
