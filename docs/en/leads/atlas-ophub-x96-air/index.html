X96 Air: turn the TV box into an Armbian server

The goal is to convert X96 Air from an Android TV box into a small Armbian host for Docker, file services, Home Assistant or light network services.

D247 · E1 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-22 · No hands-on hardware tests

EXACT-MODEL SOURCE REVIEWED

Hardware

X96 Air listed under S905X3, with a community report for the Q1000/QCA9377 variant.

Installation route

Choose the DTB by network chip and validate Ethernet, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth from external media before installation.

Expected result

A specific Q1000/QCA9377 variant has a community report of all three network functions working.

Boundary

The result does not cover every X96 Air; Q1000 and other network/memory revisions must be separated.

Recovery and stop condition Keep known DTBs and the stock Android image; do not write eMMC when the network chip does not match.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • X96 Air units with a matching SoC and board

Not established or unsuitable
