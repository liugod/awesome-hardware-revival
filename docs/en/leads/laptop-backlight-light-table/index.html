DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The tutorial removes the broken liquid-crystal layer while retaining diffuser sheets and CCFL/LED backlight, turning a non-imaging screen assembly into a drawing light table.

Practical readiness

A panel with failed LCD but working backlight can become a tracing/drawing tool.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Broken laptop screens with intact backlight and diffuser layers
  • Modules whose original backlight driver can be retained or safely replaced

Not established or unsuitable

  • Failed backlights or badly contaminated optical films
  • Opening CCFL panels without high-voltage experience

Why this lead is useful

Avoids a video controller and uses the illumination portion when image production is no longer possible.

Should you continue?

Low-voltage LED backlights are better candidates; assess CCFL high-voltage risk first.

Why it remains E0

Backlight voltage and driver boards vary; uniformity, insulation and long-term temperature are not standardized.

Next review step

Identify LED or CCFL type, driver input, power specification, insulation and two-hour temperature rise.

Risk and stop condition

CCFL inverters generate high voltage, while broken LCD glass and metal edges can cut.
