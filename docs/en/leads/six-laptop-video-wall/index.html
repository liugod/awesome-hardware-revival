Window on the World: combine six older laptops into a video window

The project proposes PXE-booting six similar old laptops and arranging their screens as a window for inter-city video exchange and art display.

L28 · E0 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The project proposes PXE-booting six similar old laptops and arranging their screens as a window for inter-city video exchange and art display.

Practical readiness

A network-boot tiled-screen experiment for a batch of old laptops, with final completion still uncertain.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Six older laptops with similar size and brightness
  • Teams able to deploy PXE and multi-node video software

Not established or unsuitable

  • Random models with mismatched panels
  • A low-power or bezel-free production video wall

Why this lead is useful

Shows how whole machines can cooperate over the network without extracting their panels.

Should you continue?

Continue for an art experiment with a matching batch; calculate power and maintenance for regular signage.

Why it remains E0

The reviewed material is a detailed plan; final six-node synchronization, camera path, power and long-term exhibition results are not fully verified.

Next review step

Find completion media, PXE image, synchronization method, network bandwidth and measured total power.

Risk and stop condition

Do not stack old adapters and batteries in a sealed frame; remove swollen cells and provide cooling.
