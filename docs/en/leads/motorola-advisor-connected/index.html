DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author disassembles a Motorola Advisor and plans a PyBoard/MicroPython rebuild reusing the enclosure, keys, beeper and vibration motor with a replacement LCD.

Practical readiness

A useful teardown reference for the rugged enclosure and interface parts; complete function is unconfirmed.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Motorola Advisor enclosure, keys, beeper and vibration parts
  • Small MicroPython notification-terminal experiments

Not established or unsuitable

  • A universal route to continue original POCSAG service
  • Direct reuse of the undocumented original LCD

Why this lead is useful

Separates reusable parts from the difficult display, reducing blind reverse engineering.

Should you continue?

Continue with the same pager if replacing screen and mainboard is acceptable; preserve complete collectible units.

Why it remains E0

The undocumented original LCD is replaced, and the logs stop before full connected-device acceptance.

Next review step

Find the final PCB, display, AAA/lithium power design, firmware and finished demonstration.

Risk and stop condition

A lithium replacement requires appropriate charging, overcurrent and mechanical protection.
