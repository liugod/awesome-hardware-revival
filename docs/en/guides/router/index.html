Reuse an old router: verify model, partitions, and recovery first

OpenWrt or FreshTomato support depends on the exact model and hardware revision. Image names, forum bundles, and similar enclosures cannot replace model-specific install material.

Find the exact profile first

  • Record the bottom label, region, and hardware revision, then resolve the exact profile in the upstream support table or firmware selector.
  • Xiaomi AX9000, AX3000T, 4A Gigabit, and Redmi AX6S are different targets. A shared brand does not make their images interchangeable.

Separate upgrade from first install

  • sysupgrade, factory, and initramfs images serve different purposes. Follow only the exact device page's first-install instructions.
  • A community roundup can discover a model, but it does not replace official device Info, partition layout, boot method, and recovery steps.
  • FreshTomato applies only to devices in its support scope. Do not infer support for an unlisted model.

Prepare recovery before choosing a job

  • Save stock configuration and required partitions, then confirm that rescue mode, serial access, or the vendor recovery tool matches the revision.
  • Verify wired networking, management access, reboot, and rollback before adding routing, Wi-Fi, or extra services.
  • KitAgain has not compared throughput, coverage, power, or long-term stability on hardware.
