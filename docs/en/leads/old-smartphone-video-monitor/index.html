DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author uses an old Samsung S5 as the camera endpoint and views a pet from another phone through a third-party app and account available at the time.

Practical readiness

A spare phone with working camera and Wi-Fi can provide temporary pet monitoring, though the specific app route may be obsolete.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Spare phones with functioning camera, Wi-Fi and app installation
  • Short-term noncritical indoor observation

Not established or unsuitable

  • Security-critical surveillance or covert recording
  • Unattended long-term use of unsafe batteries or unpatched systems

Why this lead is useful

Demonstrates a no-extra-camera pilot while exposing dependence on a cloud service.

Should you continue?

Pilot briefly with a local auditable app; use maintained devices for critical monitoring.

Why it remains E0

The Eyecura service and store availability need fresh verification; privacy, encryption, background reliability and continuous charging are untested.

Next review step

Confirm current app availability, data path, LAN/cloud mode, permissions, reboot recovery and charge limits.

Risk and stop condition

Respect privacy and avoid recording others without consent; inspect aged batteries for swelling and heat before continuous charging.
