Decide before you build

  • Current stage The tutorial removes the broken liquid-crystal layer while retaining diffuser sheets and CCFL/LED backlight, turning a non-imaging screen assembly into a drawing light table.
  • Practical readiness A panel with failed LCD but working backlight can become a tracing/drawing tool.
  • User value Avoids a video controller and uses the illumination portion when image production is no longer possible.
  • Follow-up recommendation Low-voltage LED backlights are better candidates; assess CCFL high-voltage risk first.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Broken laptop screens with intact backlight and diffuser layers
  • Modules whose original backlight driver can be retained or safely replaced

Not established or unsuitable

  • Failed backlights or badly contaminated optical films
  • Opening CCFL panels without high-voltage experience

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • Why it remains E0 Backlight voltage and driver boards vary; uniformity, insulation and long-term temperature are not standardized.
  • Next review step Identify LED or CCFL type, driver input, power specification, insulation and two-hour temperature rise.
  • Risk and stop condition CCFL inverters generate high voltage, while broken LCD glass and metal edges can cut.
