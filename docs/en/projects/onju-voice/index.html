Practical decision notes

  • What was holding it back The Google Nest Mini's original motherboard and services limit custom voice pipelines.
  • What it can do afterwards The enclosure and some acoustic components are retained, while a custom ESP32-S3 motherboard connects external voice and model services.
  • Assessment The author's original 2023 X post and later explanation confirm the board-replacement route. The post describes a voice-agent scenario, not a language model running inside the Nest Mini itself. The original version is no longer actively maintained and is useful as a hardware-reuse case study. Onju v2 is a successor in the same family; an old video does not prove every v2 feature.
  • A good fit if People willing to buy or make a PCB, disassemble the speaker and maintain external voice services.
  • Skip this route if People expecting a downloaded program to jailbreak the original device or all inference to run inside the speaker.

Checks after the build

  • Check the Nest Mini generation, PCB revision and bill of materials.
  • Verify microphone, speaker, network and server separately.
  • Test complete conversations and behavior without a network; keep the original motherboard.
