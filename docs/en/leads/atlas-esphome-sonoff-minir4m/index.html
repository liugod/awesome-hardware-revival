Exact-model review

  • Hardware ESP32-C3, 4 MB flash, one relay, external input and button; the original chip is flash-locked for Matter certification.
  • Installation route Upstream says direct reflashing is impossible; the documented route replaces the IC with a new ESP32-C3 containing 4 MB integrated flash.
  • Expected result After a successful chip swap, ESPHome can control the relay, external switch, button and blue LED.
  • Boundary This is chip-level rework rather than ordinary serial flashing, with high soldering and mains risk.
  • Recovery and stop condition Stop without hot-air rework, chip programming and board-inspection skills; the original locked chip cannot be software-unlocked by this route.
