Sony PRS-700 with Pico: update reader content through SD storage

The author plans to let a Pico rewrite EPUB/PDF files on an SD card so an old Sony PRS-700 can display changing text; the work remains at research and parts-gathering stage.

L11 · E0 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author plans to let a Pico rewrite EPUB/PDF files on an SD card so an old Sony PRS-700 can display changing text; the work remains at research and parts-gathering stage.

Practical readiness

It is currently an idea for file injection on an older reader, not a buildable conversion.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • The author's Sony PRS-700 research target
  • Developers willing to characterize SD-card behavior

Not established or unsuitable

  • Users needing a ready information display
  • Assuming all readers access removable storage identically

Why this lead is useful

Preserves a route that may avoid direct high-voltage panel driving while exposing the unresolved assumptions.

Should you continue?

Continue for protocol research; choose a demonstrated route for a dependable display.

Why it remains E0

There is no completed wiring, code, refresh demonstration or stability result, and page-by-page SD reads are still an assumption.

Next review step

Wait for a real file-update experiment, power design and reproducible code before treating it as a prototype.

Risk and stop condition

Do not treat the plan as a working guide; verify voltage, charging and protection before battery or shared-power work.
