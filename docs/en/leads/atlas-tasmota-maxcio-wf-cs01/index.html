DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The goal is to configure Tasmota for Maxcio WF-CS01 so its switch, light, plug or sensor can be controlled locally through the web UI, MQTT or a home-automation system.

Practical readiness

A real model/template starting point, but not safe to apply until the current hardware revision is confirmed.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Devices whose exact model is WF-CS01
  • Tasmota users able to verify the controller and template revision

Not established or unsuitable

  • Products with a different model or regional electrical version
  • Devices without a safe disassembly or stock-firmware recovery route

Why this lead is useful

Provides a model-level template entry instead of forcing users to guess GPIO assignments from generic flashing guides.

Should you continue?

Continue when model, chip and template agree; stop for a changed controller or an upstream unsupported warning.

Why it remains E0

Model metadata was read from the template repository, but the current retail revision and controller were not verified and no hardware was flashed.

Next review step

Match the model, regional standard, controller and flashing instructions, including any disassembly, serial access or module replacement.

Risk and stop condition

Disconnect and isolate mains hardware and use qualified help when needed. Do not infer the internal controller from the enclosure alone.
