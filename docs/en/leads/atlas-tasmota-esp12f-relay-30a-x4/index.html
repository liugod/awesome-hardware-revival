ESP-12F 5V/7-28V 4 Channel 30A (ESP12F_Relay_30A X4): flash Tasmota for local control

The goal is to configure Tasmota for ESP-12F 5V/7-28V 4 Channel 30A (ESP12F_Relay_30A X4) so its switch, light, plug or sensor can be controlled locally through the web UI, MQTT or a home-automation system.

EXACT-MODEL SOURCE REVIEWED

Hardware

ESP-12F with four nominal 30 A relays and 5 V or 7–28 V board power.

Installation route

Solder the supplied headers, flash Tasmota over serial and apply the four-channel template.

Expected result

Provides a four-channel local relay controller.

Boundary

A 30 A relay marking does not certify PCB traces, terminals, cooling or the enclosure for continuous current.

Recovery and stop condition Test each channel at low voltage and low load; do not use high current without enclosure, fuse and terminal calculations.
