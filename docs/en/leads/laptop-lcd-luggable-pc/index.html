DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author pairs a 17-inch LCD from a GPU-failed laptop with a model-specific controller and retired desktop parts to complete a luggable Mark I computer.

Practical readiness

A matching LCD can become a standard-video display and part of a custom all-in-one computer.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • The author's Dell Inspiron E1705 panel or LCDs with matched controllers
  • Still-functional retired ATX/desktop components

Not established or unsuitable

  • Buying controllers by screen size alone
  • Users seeking a thin, low-power portable computer

Why this lead is useful

Includes real experience matching a controller to a panel and integrating a complete system.

Should you continue?

Continue if the exact panel number is known and size is acceptable; bench-test before building an enclosure.

Why it remains E0

The controller must match the exact panel number; the author later moved to a new-screen Mark II and stopped developing the salvaged version.

Next review step

Record panel number, controller firmware, backlight type, native resolution and power specifications.

Risk and stop condition

CCFL backlight boards generate high voltage; enclose bare panels, sharp metal and power electronics.
