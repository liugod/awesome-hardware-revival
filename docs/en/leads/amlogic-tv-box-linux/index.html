Run Armbian on an older Amlogic TV box as a light server

The Armbian forum FAQ explicitly frames TV-box use as a community experiment and recommends headless Linux-server roles rather than desktop replacement.

L36 · E0 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The Armbian forum FAQ explicitly frames TV-box use as a community experiment and recommends headless Linux-server roles rather than desktop replacement.

Practical readiness

Some boxes can boot from SD/USB as low-cost servers, often with incomplete peripherals.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Amlogic boxes with same-board forum reports
  • Experiments needing Ethernet, USB and headless services only

Not established or unsuitable

  • Treating a retail name as one hardware configuration
  • Primary systems needing official updates and complete wireless/video acceleration

Why this lead is useful

The forum states both sensible use and failure boundaries, preventing TV boxes from being mistaken for supported SBCs.

Should you continue?

Continue with serial access/backup and experimental expectations; use a supported SBC for important services.

Why it remains E0

Identical retail shells may contain different eMMC/NAND, Wi-Fi and boards, and these are not officially supported Armbian devices.

Next review step

Open the case to identify SoC, storage and board, preserve Android firmware, and verify DTB/boot chain for one exact variant.

Risk and stop condition

A wrong DTB or eMMC write can brick the box; cooling and cheap power adapters also need long-term tests.
