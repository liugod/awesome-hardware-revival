Decide before you build

  • Current stage The community has maintained this route for years, but knowledge is spread across first posts and long reply chains rather than one installer.
  • Practical readiness It becomes an advanced route only after verifying the exact model, SoC, stock U-Boot, serial access, NAND layout and current rootfs.
  • User value Provides Debian, kernel and boot-chain clues for unsupported NAS hardware; model-specific recovery experience is its strongest value.
  • Follow-up recommendation Continue with serial tools, Linux experience and complete backups; do not follow it if you need a one-click installer.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Kirkwood devices with a dedicated thread or confirmed boot report
  • NSA325, GoFlex Home and selected Pogoplug or Synology models as thread-review starting points
  • Spare NAS devices with USB-TTL, saved boot logs and flash backups

Not established or unsuitable

  • Treating every Kirkwood NAS as the same model
  • Mixing U-Boot environment values across brands or NAND layouts
  • Devices with no serial access, no backup and irreplaceable data

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • Why it remains E0 There is no universal image for every NAS. Model, SoC, stock U-Boot, serial access, NAND layout and current downloads require thread-by-thread review.
  • Next review step Review one primary thread each for NSA325, GoFlex Home and DS211, recording current rootfs, U-Boot, checksums and recovery.
  • Risk and stop condition Some routes overwrite stock firmware; do not write flash without serial access and a verified backup.
