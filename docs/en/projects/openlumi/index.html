Practical decision notes

  • What was holding it back Old Xiaomi or Aqara gateways retain useful hardware such as Zigbee, but vendor software limits their uses.
  • What it can do afterwards Modifying the system on specified models brings the gateway into locally maintained smart-home services.
  • Assessment The listed DGNWG05LM, mieu01 EU and ZHWG11LM do not imply support for the similarly named DGNWG02LM. An original rootfs backup contains device-specific information; someone else's image cannot replace your backup. The real threshold is not finding a repository but identifying the exact version and preserving irreplaceable device data.
  • A good fit if People with an exact supported model who can follow backup, wiring and system-inspection instructions.
  • Skip this route if People identifying models only by the enclosure, lacking original backups, or expecting to reuse someone else's image directly.

Checks after the build
