Gosund WO1: convert it to a local ESPHome device

The goal is clear: use an ESPHome configuration for Gosund WO1, reduce dependence on the vendor cloud and connect it to a local system such as Home Assistant.

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The goal is clear: use an ESPHome configuration for Gosund WO1, reduce dependence on the vendor cloud and connect it to a local system such as Home Assistant.

Practical readiness

A model-specific configuration starting point, not a flash-ready guarantee until the chip and board revision match.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Devices whose exact model is Gosund WO1
  • ESPHome users prepared to verify the chip, PCB and flashing route

Not established or unsuitable

  • Lookalike products whose controller cannot be identified
  • Mains devices without a safe disassembly and isolation setup

Why this lead is useful

Maps a scattered ESPHome configuration to a concrete model so a user can decide whether deeper research and disassembly are worthwhile.

Should you continue?

Continue when the exact model and chip match; stop at research when any hardware revision remains unclear.

Why it remains E0

This review confirmed that the upstream device database contains a page for this model, but has not checked every chip, PCB revision, flashing method or feature on that page.

Next review step

Open the model page and verify the controller, pins, hardware revision and flashing access, then find a matching-PCB report and recovery route.

Risk and stop condition

Disconnect mains-powered devices before opening them and never probe an exposed energized device. Identical product names may hide different chips or PCBs.
