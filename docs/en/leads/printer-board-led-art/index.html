Turn a vintage printer character board into LED art

The author modifies a DEC LA30 terminal character-generator board so counters scan its ROM and seven LEDs expose dot-matrix row data.

L20 · E0 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author modifies a DEC LA30 terminal character-generator board so counters scan its ROM and seven LEDs expose dot-matrix row data.

Practical readiness

A digital-logic and font-ROM teaching artifact with limited practical function.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • The same DEC LA30 character board or a similar identifiable ROM board
  • Learning experiments that expose old font data

Not established or unsuitable

  • A universal printer-mainboard reuse method
  • A finished device that prints or displays full text

Why this lead is useful

Preserves a way to learn early character-generation logic from a discarded board.

Should you continue?

Follow if you own the same board and want to learn; for ordinary printers salvage motors, rails and supplies first.

Why it remains E0

This is a one-off work for a particular vintage board, without a universal interface or modern replacement-parts route.

Next review step

Preserve board number, ROM type, wiring modifications and working video, and check for repeatable instructions.

Risk and stop condition

Use only a removed logic board confirmed to be low voltage; do not power an unknown complete vintage machine.
