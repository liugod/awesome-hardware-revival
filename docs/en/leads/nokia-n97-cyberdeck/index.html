DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author maps the N97 keyboard matrix, FPC and four-wire resistive touch, planning a Linux pocket computer with a Zero-form-factor SBC, custom PCB and 3.2-inch HDMI screen.

Practical readiness

Currently a strong reverse-engineering record and partial prototype, not a reproducible finished device.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Nokia N97 enclosure, keyboard and touch parts
  • Developers able to design fine-pitch PCBs and mechanical adapters

Not established or unsuitable

  • Applying directly to other Nokia sliders
  • A solder-free consumer conversion

Why this lead is useful

Published keyboard mapping and connector identification lower the cost of continuing with this exact model.

Should you continue?

Worth following for reverse engineering and custom-board work; wait for integration if you need a finished pocket computer.

Why it remains E0

Keyboard and touch are mapped, while the integrated PCB, power, screen fit and complete boot acceptance remain in progress.

Next review step

Review the latest board, keyboard-scanning firmware, battery management, HDMI-screen assembly and bootable system.

Risk and stop condition

Fine-pitch FPC, an aged battery and tight slider space leave little tolerance; do not charge an unknown original cell.
