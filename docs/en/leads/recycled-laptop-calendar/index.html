DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author uses a salvaged laptop LCD, matching controller and Raspberry Pi for a wall-mounted Google Calendar that follows phone updates.

Practical readiness

A practical household schedule display that depends on a cloud account and network.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Laptop LCDs with matched video controllers
  • Raspberry Pi or small hosts capable of a current browser

Not established or unsuitable

  • A fully offline household calendar
  • Leaving a personal primary account logged into an exposed display

Why this lead is useful

Provides a clear everyday role for an otherwise unused screen.

Should you continue?

Worth trying with a separate read-only account and recovery; use local calendars when privacy is critical.

Why it remains E0

The tutorial depends on old browser and Google behavior; authentication, kiosk mode, offline handling and recovery need current redesign.

Next review step

Verify the panel controller, a read-only calendar account, kiosk autostart, offline status and reboot recovery.

Risk and stop condition

In-wall wiring and bare controllers require low-voltage power, ventilation and fire-conscious mounting.
