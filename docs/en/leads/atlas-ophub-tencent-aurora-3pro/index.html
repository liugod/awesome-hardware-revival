Exact-model review

  • Hardware Tencent Aurora 3Pro with S905X3-B, now listed on the S905X3 stable route.
  • Installation route Test related DTBs from microSD/USB and record boot, Ethernet, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth separately.
  • Expected result Community evidence shows it can enter Armbian, but early testing lacked a fully matched dedicated DTB.
  • Boundary Issue 506 reports Wi-Fi and Bluetooth could not operate together and DTBs differed in load and network behavior.
  • Recovery and stop condition Keep stock firmware and a bootable DTB; do not install to eMMC when simultaneous Wi-Fi/Bluetooth is required and the driver conflict remains.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Tencent Aurora 3Pro units with a matching SoC and board
  • Users willing to validate non-destructively from USB or microSD first

Not established or unsuitable

  • Same-name boxes with unknown board, Wi-Fi or storage chips
  • Devices headed for direct eMMC installation without a stock backup

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • What E1 still does not prove An exact-model configuration or maintainer record was read, but KitAgain did not flash, measure or run the hardware long-term, and upstream evidence cannot guarantee an identical current retail revision.
  • Next review step Match the physical model, PCB and controller against the source, then begin with the lowest-risk validation step. Keep stock firmware and a bootable DTB; do not install to eMMC when simultaneous Wi-Fi/Bluetooth is required and the driver conflict remains.
  • Risk and stop condition Back up the stock Android and boot partitions. A wrong device tree may lose display, network or boot; do not overwrite eMMC before external-media testing.
