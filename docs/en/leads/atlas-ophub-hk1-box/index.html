Exact-model review

  • Hardware HK1 Box listed by ophub under Amlogic S905X3 on the stable-kernel route.
  • Installation route Boot from USB/microSD and verify the meson-sm1-hk1box-vontar-x3 device tree before considering eMMC installation.
  • Expected result Can serve as a small Armbian host; the linked report also discusses LED display and fan GPIO.
  • Boundary Fan, display, network and board variants exist under the same name; issue 414 concerns a specific acrylic fan model.
  • Recovery and stop condition Save the Android image and boot partitions and require stable external boot before eMMC; stop on fan failure or unsafe temperature.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • HK1 Box units with a matching SoC and board
  • Users willing to validate non-destructively from USB or microSD first

Not established or unsuitable

  • Same-name boxes with unknown board, Wi-Fi or storage chips
  • Devices headed for direct eMMC installation without a stock backup

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • What E1 still does not prove An exact-model configuration or maintainer record was read, but KitAgain did not flash, measure or run the hardware long-term, and upstream evidence cannot guarantee an identical current retail revision.
  • Next review step Match the physical model, PCB and controller against the source, then begin with the lowest-risk validation step. Save the Android image and boot partitions and require stable external boot before eMMC; stop on fan failure or unsafe temperature.
  • Risk and stop condition Back up the stock Android and boot partitions. A wrong device tree may lose display, network or boot; do not overwrite eMMC before external-media testing.
