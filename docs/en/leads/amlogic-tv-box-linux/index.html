Decide before you build

  • Current stage The Armbian forum FAQ explicitly frames TV-box use as a community experiment and recommends headless Linux-server roles rather than desktop replacement.
  • Practical readiness Some boxes can boot from SD/USB as low-cost servers, often with incomplete peripherals.
  • User value The forum states both sensible use and failure boundaries, preventing TV boxes from being mistaken for supported SBCs.
  • Follow-up recommendation Continue with serial access/backup and experimental expectations; use a supported SBC for important services.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Amlogic boxes with same-board forum reports
  • Experiments needing Ethernet, USB and headless services only

Not established or unsuitable

  • Treating a retail name as one hardware configuration
  • Primary systems needing official updates and complete wireless/video acceleration

Gaps, next step, and risk

  • Why it remains E0 Identical retail shells may contain different eMMC/NAND, Wi-Fi and boards, and these are not officially supported Armbian devices.
  • Next review step Open the case to identify SoC, storage and board, preserve Android firmware, and verify DTB/boot chain for one exact variant.
  • Risk and stop condition A wrong DTB or eMMC write can brick the box; cooling and cheap power adapters also need long-term tests.
