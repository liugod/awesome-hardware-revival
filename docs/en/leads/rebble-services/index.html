Rebble: community services after Pebble shutdown

An official HOWTO, help center, Discord and project pages collectively support setup, an app store and selected cloud services for older Pebble watches.

L04 · E0 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

Community services are usable with a setup guide and help center. Legacy apps, phone-OS limits and newer service routes still coexist.

Practical readiness

Many Pebble watches can regain the app store and selected cloud services, but a working phone app is required; weather and dictation may require a subscription.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Working Pebble Classic, Steel, Time, Time Steel, Round and Pebble 2 families as exact-device review candidates
  • Android or iOS users able to install or sideload the Pebble app

Not established or unsuitable

  • Assuming every new phone OS can directly install the legacy app
  • Assuming every former cloud function is restored completely and for free
  • Expecting cloud services to fix broken buttons, screens or batteries

Why this lead is useful

Helps decide what software value remains, how connection works, which functions require subscriptions and where to troubleshoot.

Should you continue?

Continue if the watch hardware works and phone-side compatibility work is acceptable; value is limited for broken hardware or when sideloading is unacceptable.

Why it remains E0

New Pebble app and service paths now coexist. KitAgain has not reconciled legacy Android/iOS versions, watch families, paid services and firmware combinations.

Next review step

Map current pairing, app installation, service dependencies and exit paths for Pebble Classic, Time and Pebble 2 across phone platforms.

Risk and stop condition

Do not start with a factory reset. Preserve the currently working phone app, firmware and pairing state first.
