DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

After a Galaga mini cabinet fails, the author removes its electronics, fits a same-size LCD and rebuilds game/control functions with Raspberry Pi and Arduino components.

Practical readiness

Keeps the enclosure and controls useful as a retro machine while replacing original electronics.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • The author's class of Galaga mini cabinet
  • Low-voltage mini arcades with failed boards but usable shell/buttons

Not established or unsuitable

  • Preserving original-board collector value
  • Directly copying the method to full-size CRT arcades

Why this lead is useful

Shows when replacing a difficult display is more practical than reverse engineering it.

Should you continue?

Follow for a low-value failed unit; diagnose and preserve rare original hardware first.

Why it remains E0

The original LCD was intentionally not reverse engineered; exact cabinet revision, button wiring, software image and complete files need further review.

Next review step

Confirm exact machine, replacement display, input mapping, lawful game-content source and power-loss recovery.

Risk and stop condition

Verify that the unit is low-voltage adapter powered; do not apply this directly to full-size mains/CRT cabinets.
