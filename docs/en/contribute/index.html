Contribute a reliable record

Keep successes, failures and unsupported conditions.

What to include

  • Project ID, exact model, hardware revision, firmware and region
  • Upstream version, operation date and original instructions
  • Working and failed functions, recovery results and verifiable evidence
  • Do not publish unique serial numbers, accounts, tokens or secrets

How reports enter the catalog

Submissions begin as unverified. Editors check sources and exact device conditions, then update both languages. No E3 upgrade without independent evidence. Failure reports are valuable too.
