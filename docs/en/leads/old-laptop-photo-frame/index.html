Turn a 2006 laptop into a digital photo frame

The author mounts an older laptop motherboard and display in a 12×18-inch frame, using MX Linux, Feh and VNC for an automatic slideshow.

L24 · E0 · Sources reviewed 2026-10-21 · No hands-on hardware tests

DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The author mounts an older laptop motherboard and display in a 12×18-inch frame, using MX Linux, Feh and VNC for an automatic slideshow.

Practical readiness

It can display photos and be maintained remotely, but high-frequency noise may make the result unacceptable.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Older x86 laptops with working motherboard and LCD
  • Systems able to boot lightweight Linux from USB

Not established or unsuitable

  • Rooms with listeners sensitive to high-frequency noise
  • Long-term wall mounting of unprotected boards

Why this lead is useful

Documents both working function and a real unresolved defect without hiding it.

Should you continue?

Test noise, temperature and reboot recovery as a complete laptop before framing it.

Why it remains E0

Function is complete, but the author records an unresolved roughly 13.5 kHz whine; long-term power, heat and wall-mount fire behavior are unmeasured.

Next review step

Identify the whine source and verify automatic reboot, remote maintenance, temperature and idle power.

Risk and stop condition

Remove the aged battery, cover exposed electronics and provide cooling and flame-conscious wall mounting.
