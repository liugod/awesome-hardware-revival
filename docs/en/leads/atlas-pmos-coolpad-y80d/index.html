DECIDE BEFORE YOU BUILD

Current stage

The goal is to move Coolpad Y80D beyond its abandoned vendor OS and try postmarketOS as a Linux terminal, test device or dedicated controller.

Practical readiness

A model-level Linux porting entry, not a daily-driver recommendation until the device status table is reviewed.

Device fit and limits

Known fit or worth checking

  • Devices whose exact model is Coolpad Y80D
  • Users who accept missing functions and are willing to test a port

Not established or unsuitable

  • Primary devices requiring full camera, calling, GPS and related functions
  • Devices without backups or a known recovery/download mode

Why this lead is useful

Connects a concrete old device to its upstream porting entry so boot and feature maturity can be checked quickly.

Should you continue?

Continue when the device page boots and its key functions match the intended use; watch and wait for placeholder or not-booting ports.

Why it remains E0

This pass only confirmed a model entry in the postmarketOS device index. Boot status, feature matrix and current installation steps have not yet been read.

Next review step

Open the exact device page and check its category, kernel route, flashing method, display, touch, Wi-Fi, audio and cellular status.

Risk and stop condition

Unlocking and flashing may erase data or prevent boot. Confirm a recovery image, download mode and exact codename first.
